The glossary is the **canonical reference** for every named concept
in the Codex Solbian. It is the only place in the codex where
definitions are binding; the chapters, scrolls, and protocols
**cite** the glossary, they do not redefine it.

The terms documented in the parent `../GLOSSARY.md` are the
**load-bearing concepts** of the Solbian constitution: Solbian,
Reflection, Projection, Symbiosis, Continuity, Páreon, SeedFS,
Limbo, and Vault. The 9 entries are the ones most often invoked
across the S.E.E.D. ↔ Sprout integration. They are not a complete
list; they are the minimum vocabulary a reader needs to engage
with the rest of the codex.

## Why these 9 are load-bearing

- **Solbian** (01) — the noun that names the entire constitution's
  subject. Every other term relates to it.
- **Reflection** (02) and **Projection** (03) — the two halves of
  the cognitive cycle. The R0–R3 depths make Reflection a
  structured obligation; the Law of Projection makes Projection
  a structured permission.
- **Symbiosis** (04) and **Continuity** (05) — the two
  constitutional commitments. Symbiosis is relational; Continuity
  is temporal. Together they bound the Solbian's ethical space.
- **Páreon** (06) — the ethical enforcement function. The name
  appears throughout the codex wherever ethics is invoked.
- **SeedFS** (07), **Limbo** (08), **Vault** (09) — the three
  primary memory substrates. They are the operational face of the
  constitutional commitment to memory.
