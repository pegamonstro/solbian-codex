## A. Corpus genre terms

### Codex
- **Operational:** The Solbian intellectual body-of-work *as a project object* (artefacts + working representation), not a single file.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Codex Machina; a prompt pack; “whatever is in `/workspace` today.”  
- **Standing:** Name of project / corpus — **NOT** auto-CANONICAL content.

### Law
- **Operational:** A normative artefact row/object inside a **named catalogue** (A / B / B′ / C) with evidenced schema/id.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Project Constitution sections; Protocols; “law” in ordinary legal systems; Working gloss prose that *sounds* mandatory.  
- **Standing:** HISTORICAL catalogues remain unmerged; “Law” ≠ single schema across sets (**NOT ESTABLISHED** as one meaning).

### Canon
- **Operational:** In this corpus, usually the **Five Ethical Canons** (ETHICS SOURCE extract) — a named set, not “any important text.”  
- **Do not confuse with:** “canonical” (epistemic status); Chapters; Scrolls.  
- **Standing:** SOURCE bodies exist; Working elevation **NOT ESTABLISHED** (JD-only).

### Covenant
- **Operational:** Binding-pact genre artefacts — esp. Triadic Covenant + pillar cluster in ETHICS; Chapter 04 as HISTORICAL sibling (merge **NOT ESTABLISHED**).  
- **Do not confuse with:** Canons; Laws catalogues; ordinary contract law.  
- **Standing:** SOURCE / HISTORICAL sibling — not auto-elevated.

### Protocol
- **Operational:** Procedural/runtime-facing artefact set (Gitea five vs Mac eight surfaces). Distinct corpus from Laws even when titles overlap.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Set A objects *titled* “Protocol of …” but typed `kind:law`; “network protocol.”  
- **Standing:** 5↔8 CONFLICTED/unmerged; binding-to-Set-C = PROPOSED INTERPRETATION.

### Scroll
- **Operational:** Doctrinal/developmental expansion unit; MD and sref inventories are **not** ordinal-aligned by default.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Chapters; Laws; “scroll” as UI metaphor.  
- **Standing:** HISTORICAL multi-surface; anomalies preserved.

### Chapter
- **Operational:** Discursive/narrative unit (title inventory; some bodies evidenced).  
- **Do not confuse with:** Scrolls; Constitution “sections.”  
- **Standing:** HISTORICAL; Ch.06–08→A:01–03 condensation VERIFIED.

### Annotation
- **Operational:** Marginal / interpretive note attached to artefacts; lower authority than the annotated Word unless separately elevated.  
- **Do not confuse with:** SYNTHESIS essays; CANONICAL amendments.  
- **Standing:** Editorial layer — default non-canonical.

### Dialogue
- **Operational:** JD↔Solace (and related) conversation SOURCE — Journey material; private-by-default.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Working Codex narrative; Law text; publication.  
- **Standing:** SOURCE — not doctrine.

## B. Epistemic / editorial status terms

### Working Codex
- **Operational:** Current readable representation for study/development (`CURRENT_WORKING_CODEX/`). Hypothesis under audit.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Archive originals; CANONICAL doctrine.  
- **Standing:** WORKING / SYNTHESIS pointers — **CANONICAL: NO**.

### Historical Corpus
- **Operational:** Recovered/preserved artefacts and inventories with provenance (hashes, paths, schemas) — immutable intent for Layer-1 sources.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Working restatements; “old therefore true/canonical.”  
- **Standing:** HISTORICAL / SOURCE.

### Synthesis
- **Operational:** Explicit interpretive joining of sources, marked as such.  
- **Do not confuse with:** SOURCE quotation; CANONICAL adoption.  
- **Standing:** SYNTHESIS — must stay fenced.

### Candidate
- **Operational:** Draft artefact offered for review (`epistemic_status` PROPOSED/CANDIDATE).  
- **Do not confuse with:** Already-binding Law/Canon.  
- **Standing:** Pre-review.

### Canonical
- **Operational:** Explicit metadata/status after human (JD) elevation — **filesystem path ≠ authority**.  
- **Do not confuse with:** “Canon” (Five Canons); “seems central”; stealth authoritative prose.  
- **Standing:** **NONE** in Wave 4 scope unless JD marks otherwise.

### Provenance
- **Operational:** Traceable origin: path, hash, schema/id, citation, chronology pointers.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Justification of truth; Journey dialogue body itself.  
- **Standing:** Required for living corpus (governance) — evidentiary, not self-elevating.

### Word
- **Operational:** Current or historical *formulation artefacts* (what is said as content).  
- **Do not confuse with:** Journey; “the one true wording” without status mark.  
- **Standing:** Dual-layer term (governance) — see `WORD_AND_JOURNEY.md`.

### Journey
- **Operational:** Dialogue, disputes, revisions, alternatives, abandoned ideas, decisions trail.  
- **Do not confuse with:** Canonical text; equal authority to reviewed Word.  
- **Standing:** Preservable SOURCE/HISTORICAL — **preservation ≠ canon**.
