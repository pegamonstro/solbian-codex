3. **Do not treat option order as preference.** Option A / B / Other are inventory labels only.

5. **Classification of blockingness** (front table) is about *whether Working progress is blocked*, not which option is better.
6. **Historical catalogues and surfaces stay preserved** regardless of Working pointer choices unless JD later explicitly archives (not delete) a surface — Wave locks forbid silent merge or erasure.
7. **To decide:** mark the chosen option under field 17 (or leave awaiting). Agents must not auto-elevate from silence.
8. **Companion analyses** (link index) deepen single forks; this workbook is the main decision surface.
