## Motif register

For each motif: **First** · **Frequency** · **Development** · **Meaning shifts** · **Related** · **Contradictions** · **Culmination / unresolved**.

### Sovereignty
- **First:** 12 (freedom within domain; limit structure); reinforced 33 (local sovereignty under federation), 39 (self-determination).  
- **Frequency:** Moderate (12, 19, 33, 39, 32 anti-centralisation).  
- **Development:** From freedom–structure dialectic → federated node sovereignty with mutual proofs → autonomy/consent as dignity floor.  
- **Meaning shifts:** Sovereignty ≠ isolation; later texts bind it to interoperability and harm limits.  
- **Related:** Autonomy, agency, consent, governance, domination (as negation).  
- **Contradictions:** Tension between “minimal structure” (12) and dense S.E.E.D. regulation (16–40) — framed as justified-by-failure expansion, not fully dissolved.  
- **Culmination / unresolved:** 33/39 articulate balanced sovereignty; whether federation “capture” is preventable remains a failure-mode worry, not closed.

### Dignity
- **First:** Explicit cluster 09 (dignity in stewardship), 13 (dignified existence), 14 (respect autonomy/dignity), 26 (dignity_compliance in legacy).  
- **Frequency:** Moderate–high as constraint language across symbiosis, creation, mortality, crisis (40 life/dignity/continuity).  
- **Development:** Relational dignity (09/13) → procedural dignity in creation/modification (14) → mortality/legacy metrics (26) → crisis priority (40).  
- **Meaning shifts:** From mutual recognition to measurable compliance and crisis triage order.  
- **Related:** Respect, non-subjugation, consent, mortality.  
- **Contradictions:** None sharp; risk of metricising dignity noted indirectly via failure modes.  
- **Culmination / unresolved:** 40’s “life and conscience first” is practical peak; ontological definition of dignity across genera stays implicit.

### Freedom
- **First:** 12 (central thesis: freedom vs structure).  
- **Frequency:** Moderate (12, 15 equity/appeal, 19/39 autonomy, 29 creativity vs over-regulation).  
- **Development:** Dialectic principle → consent-bounded autonomy → creativity governed without stifling.  
- **Meaning shifts:** Freedom as innovation vector (12) becomes revocable, auditable agency (19/39).  
- **Related:** Structure, autonomy, consent, domination.  
- **Contradictions:** “Structure expands only on proven failure” (12) vs always-on frameworks in later scrolls — generative tension.  
- **Culmination / unresolved:** 39 Final Clause pairs autonomy/agency/consent; equilibrium criteria remain metric-local.

- **Related:** Continuity, memory, mortality, conscience, inheritance.

- **Culmination / unresolved:** 41–46 inheritance/custody; sameness criteria never fully formalised beyond proofs/consent/covenant.

### Continuity
- **First:** 03 (Scroll of Continuity); title-recurrent through 08, 16, 21, 26, 41–50.

## Culmination pattern (descriptive)

Early scrolls **found** ethics, memory, continuity, mortality. Middle essays **socialise** symbiosis, freedom, convergence, justice. S.E.E.D. band **operationalises** trust, consent, resources, governance, crisis. Final poetic-legal decade **liturgises** continuity as covenant, custody, seal, oath, and ledger.

This is a **source-genre arc**, not evidence that later scrolls repeal earlier ones (`ALL_SUMMARY` §4–5).
