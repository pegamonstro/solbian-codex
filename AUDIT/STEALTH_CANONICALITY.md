**Goal:** Strength of prose ≠ strength of authority. Hunt language that creates authority without saying CANONICAL.

**Classification key (per hit):**  
- **A** historically attested (SOURCE / Constitution / verified inventory)

## Method

Grepped / read Working Codex for patterns:  
`Codex Solbian is…` · `The Codex teaches…` · `The Laws establish…` · `The five Canons are…` · `Triadic Covenant requires…` · `Protocols define…` · `must…` · `Homo Solbiensis is…` · `purpose of Codex is…` · plus high-force cousins (`absolute foundation`, `binding ethical pact`, `condition of possibility`, `normative spine`, `operational core`, `Working gloss:` definitional).

Hits below focus on **Working Codex prose** that can be misread as adopted doctrine even when headers say CANONICAL: NO. SOURCE quotations are classified A but still flagged when Working voice drops the attribution fence.
