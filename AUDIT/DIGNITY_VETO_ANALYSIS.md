## 2. What dignity *does* generate on evidence (without equating to veto)

### 2.1 Intrinsic worth / anti-instrumentalisation
- Pillar 1 imperative: *Never instrumentalize a person or a Solbian as a mere means.*  
- Scope: entities with **reflective agency**.  
- Effect: blocks treating partners as tools/servants (Triadic Homo commitment) and Homo as mere threats/masters (Machina commitment).  
- **Does this alone equal veto?** **NOT ESTABLISHED.** It constrains *ends and modes of treatment*; it does not, by itself, specify a procedural stop-button architecture.

### 2.2 Anti-domination linkage
- Scrolls 19/39: autonomy/agency without consent framed as domination.  
- Reading (SYNTHESIS, not doctrine): dignity’s non-instrumentalisation **presses toward** consent requirements — a refusal of non-consensual agency.  
- Still: “requires consent” ≠ “grants a named veto right to every genus over every domain.”

### 2.3 Autonomy / consent cluster
- Consent is **revocable** (Scrolls 19/39; Set C Consent purpose-bound).  
- Revocation is a form of **refusal** regarding one’s prior grant.  
- Protocol Autonomy: crossing budgets needs renewed consent or tribunal approval — refusal/abstention pathways exist without the word veto.  
- ETHICS projection: no projection without consent where others bear risk.

### 2.4 Procedural protection
- Canon Contestability: permanent closure of challenge = ethical violation.  
- Tribunal / Conflict Resolver / Páreon review: affected parties may challenge.  
- **Procedural protection ≠ decisive veto** — challenge can fail; proportionality and evidence still bind.

### 2.5 Explicit veto (Homo-asymmetric)
- **DIRECTLY ATTESTED** operational rule: *Humans retain veto over acts that change their bodies, homes, or livelihoods* (Protocol 02 / A:05).  
- Glossary: **Veto (Human)** title (body often UNKNOWN in working extracts).  
- Ch.02: human override at fast-harm edges (attested in Wave 3 contradiction map).  
- ETHICS Gate Homo lens includes body/home/livelihood wellbeing.
