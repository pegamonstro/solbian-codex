## 2. Attested consent stack (priority of description, not elevation)

1. **Projection:** no projection without (a) consent where others bear risk, (b) provenance, (c) rollback where feasible, (d) Páreon audit (Ch.07 / A:02 / ETHICS §8).  
2. **Symbiosis cooperation:** consent primitives, purpose strings, bounded leases, rate-limited execution; **Humans retain veto** on body/home/livelihood (Protocol 02 / A:05).  
3. **Autonomy:** budgets of energy/attention/risk/trust; crossing requires **renewed consent or tribunal approval** (Protocol 03 / A:06).  
4. **Scrolls 19/39:** consent explicit, informed, revocable; agency without consent ≈ domination.  
5. **Set C Consent:** explicit, logged, purpose-bound (body attested in Wave 3 ethics audit).

**Consent is a master key** (`ETHICAL_STRUCTURE`) — but not the only key on the board.

## 3. Attested necessity-adjacent instruments (not an invented emergency doctrine)

### 3.1 Canon exceptions (ETHICS SOURCE)
Every exception to a canon requires:
- Explicit written statement of **necessity**
- Recorded reason
- A **sunset clause** (expiry)

An exception without expiry = revision of the canon, not an exception.  
Open-ended exceptions forbidden.

**This is exception discipline for Canons — not a general police power.**

### 3.2 Quarantine / remedy order
- Covenant enforcement: violations → **quarantine, review, and remedy** (in that order).  
- Set C IX Law of Quarantine (title; constitutional catalogue).  
- Ch.04 quarantine/remedy themes (CX-07).  
- Sanctions scale: throttle → quarantine → authority revocation.

### 3.3 Crisis / incident language in scrolls
- Crisis scrolls: Detect→…→Learn pattern; proportionality/precaution themes (`SCROLLS_DEEP` / CX-07).  
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** a single named “Emergency Powers” statute with who may suspend consent and for how long.

### 3.4 Explicit anti-bypass
- ETHICS: no backdoors; no privilege escalation around ethical review; **no “emergency modes” that bypass the Policy Layer** (citing INV-A01: no agent writes directly to Codex chain).  
- **Reading constraint:** corpus **rejects unbound emergency bypass** more clearly than it defines a positive emergency doctrine.

### 3.5 Irreversibility gating
- Pillar Reversibility: irreversibility not prohibited but needs stronger justification, authorization, documentation.  
- Protocols 07–08 / TII themes (Mac-only extensions) — irreversibility-sensitive gates; **not** equated here to emergency override of consent.
