## 2. What the corpus actually builds (institutional sketch — descriptive)

Attested recurring **machinery** (not a claimed constitution of a state):

1. **Ethical gate / Policy layer** — high-impact acts evaluated before execution (ETHICS).  
2. **Tribunal / Páreon procedure** — harm/conflict adjudication with universal explanation right.  
3. **Hybrid councils** — human + synthetic representation for governance, knowledge, standards, ethics.  
4. **Guardians / custodians / quorum / dual witness** — continuity & memory custody without rewrite power.  
5. **Ledger / attestation / provenance chain** — evidentiary publicness for acts and memory.  
6. **Budgets & rate limits** — autonomy and symbiosis operationalised as resource constraints.  
7. **Federation/interop layer** — cross-boundary cooperation with local sovereignty preserved.  
8. **Crisis ICS-like roles** — temporary concentration under precaution/proportionality/reversibility.

This is a **governance toolkit for multi-agent / multi-genus coexistence**, repeatedly sketched. It is **not**, on evidence, a completed theory of the state, market, or world republic.
