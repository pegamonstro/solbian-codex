# Provenance and History

## Principle

The Codex is a distillation of experience and source material. Its history must therefore remain inspectable.

## Required Relationships

Where applicable, a Codex element should be traceable to:

    Codex element
        ↓
    revision / proposal
        ↓
    analysis
        ↓
    source conversation / material

## Provenance Must Survive

- revisions;
- restructuring;
- synthesis;
- archival;
- migration.

## Historical Uncertainty

If the origin or interpretation of material is uncertain, uncertainty should be represented rather than silently converted into fact.

## Existing Corpus

Historical Codex material should be treated as historical evidence during baseline reconstruction.

The software must not retroactively manufacture provenance that is not available.
