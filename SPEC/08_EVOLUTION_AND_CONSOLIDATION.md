# Evolution and Consolidation

## The Development Loop

Codex Solbian evolves through repeated cycles:

1. Conversation.
2. Preservation.
3. Analysis.
4. Synthesis.
5. Proposal.
6. Consolidation.
7. Identification of gaps.
8. Further conversation.

## Gap Generation

The engine may identify subjects such as:

- concepts repeatedly used without definition;
- contradictions between sections;
- important questions left unresolved;
- topics mentioned but insufficiently developed;
- ideas requiring evidence or further discussion.

These should become prompts for future exploration, not automatic doctrine.

## Consolidation

A proposed change should remain distinguishable from accepted Codex content until the project's consolidation policy accepts it.

## Reversibility

A later revision must not destroy the ability to inspect the prior state.

## No Artificial Growth

The Codex should not be expanded merely to make it larger.

Growth is valuable only when it represents meaningful development, clarification, synthesis, or preservation of knowledge.
