# Security and Integrity

## Objectives

Phase I must protect:

- source conversations;
- Codex content;
- provenance;
- credentials;
- private communications.

## Integrity

The system should make accidental or silent destruction of source material difficult.

## Separation

Source material and derived Codex content should remain logically distinguishable.

## Credentials

External service credentials must not be embedded in source content or Codex documents.

## Privacy

Potential future external sources such as IRC, WhatsApp, and email may contain material belonging to other people.

Their ingestion is therefore deferred until identity, consent, retention, access, and provenance requirements are explicitly defined.

This is another reason not to implement multi-source ingestion in Phase I.
