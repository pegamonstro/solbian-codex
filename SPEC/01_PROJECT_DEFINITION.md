# Project Definition

## Name

Codex Solbian — Phase I

## Objective

Build a small, dependable software environment for the continuing development of Codex Solbian through conversation between JD and Solace.

## Primary outcome

A working loop in which:

- JD and Solace converse;
- conversations are durably preserved;
- the Codex Engine analyses them;
- relevant knowledge and philosophical development are identified;
- proposed Codex changes are produced;
- Codex Solbian can be browsed independently from the conversation;
- provenance connects Codex material back to its sources.

## Initial user surfaces

Phase I should expose two principal surfaces:

1. **Solace** — the living conversation.
2. **Codex Solbian** — a read-only presentation of the current Codex.

Administrative inspection may exist where necessary for development, but should not become a third major product surface prematurely.

## Success criterion

The system can ingest the existing Codex corpus, preserve its structure and history, support ongoing JD ↔ Solace conversations, produce understandable proposed syntheses, and allow the resulting Codex to be inspected without obscuring or destroying provenance.
